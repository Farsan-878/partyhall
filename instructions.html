Party Hall Instructions & Policies

Please review our party hall booking guidelines, decoration restrictions, and cake policies to ensure a smooth celebration.

Party Hall Instructions

Booking schedules, setup grace periods, and hygiene rules

1
Advance Booking: The party hall must be booked in advance to avoid scheduling conflicts.
2
Setup Grace Time: A preparation and decoration time of 15–30 minutes before the event is allowed at management discretion.
3
Extra Time Billing: If the event exceeds the booked time, additional charges of ₹1,000/hr will be calculated accordingly.
4
Sound Levels: Music volume must be maintained at a moderate level in respect of cafe patrons.
5
Cleanliness & Tidiness: Guests are kindly requested to maintain basic cleanliness and dispose of waste in designated bins.
6
Safety & Property: Cafe Coffea is not liable for loss or damage to personal belongings brought by guests.
7
Child Supervision: Children must be supervised by adults throughout the celebration.
8
Strict Prohibition: Smoking, alcohol consumption, and fire crackers/sparklers are strictly prohibited inside the premises.

Decoration Policy

Wall protection, tape guidelines, and damage assessments

1
Allowed Decorations: Basic decorations such as balloons, banners, and backdrops are permitted.
2
Wall Safety: Do not use nails, screws, strong adhesive tape, or staplers that damage paint or wall panels.
3
Approved Tapes Only: Only removable paper tape or masking tape may be used on designated surfaces.
4
Restricted Items: Snow sprays, party poppers with permanent dye, and smoke bombs are strictly forbidden inside the hall.
5
Prompt Removal: All personal decorations must be removed by the host immediately upon event completion.
6
Damage Inspection: If any damage occurs to lighting, furnishings, or walls, management will inspect before clearance.
7
Repair Charges: Necessary repair or replacement charges will be assessed and billed directly to the booking organizer.
8
Official Record: Management maintains an inspection record for damages and applicable fees.

Cake Policy

In-house cake pre-orders, outside cakes, and food restrictions

1
In-House Pre-Booking: Customers can pre-book exquisite custom cakes directly from Cafe Coffea.
2
Transparent Pricing: Cakes prepared by Cafe Coffea have separate pricing based on flavor, weight, and design.
3
Outside Cakes: Customers are welcome to bring their own birthday or anniversary celebration cake.
4
No Outside Food: Other outside catering, snacks, and beverages are strictly not allowed inside the venue.
5
Advance Notice: Please inform the café team at least 24 hours in advance regarding cake storage and cutting arrangements.
