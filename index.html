Guidelines for Guests

Instructions & Policies

To maintain our café's pristine ambiance and ensure a seamless experience for every guest, please review the party hall policies below.

Party Hall Rules

Duration, booking tariffs, and timing adherence

1
Standard Charge: Standard party hall charge is ₹1,000 for one hour.
2
One-Time Cleaning: Cleaning charge is ₹200 per booking (applied only once).
3
Allotted Duration: Standard party hall duration is exactly one hour per booked slot.
4
Schedule Punctuality: Customers must follow the allotted time strictly.
5
Additional Usage: Additional usage requires prior approval and applicable charges.

Decoration Policy

Wallpaper preservation, safe methods, and property care

1
Own Arrangements: Customers are allowed to arrange their own decorations.
2
Wallpaper Protection: Customers must ensure that decorations do not damage the wallpaper.
3
Property Care: Do not damage walls, furniture, lighting, fixtures or other café property.
4
Safe Methods: Use safe decoration methods (such as non-marking adhesive clips or free-standing stands) that can be removed without causing damage.
5
Customer Responsibility: Customers are responsible for damage caused during their event.
6
Inspection: If any damage occurs, the café management will inspect it.
7
Repair Charges: The necessary repair or replacement charges will be assessed and communicated to the customer.
8
Official Record: Maintain a record of damages and applicable charges.

Cake Policy

In-house cake orders, outside cakes, and food restrictions

1
In-House Pre-Booking: Customers can pre-book cakes directly from Cafe Coffea.
2
Transparent Pricing: Cakes prepared by Cafe Coffea have separate prices.
3
Outside Cakes: Customers may also bring their own cake from outside.
4
No Outside Food: Other outside food items are strictly not allowed.
5
Advance Notice: Customers should inform the café in advance about their cake requirements.
